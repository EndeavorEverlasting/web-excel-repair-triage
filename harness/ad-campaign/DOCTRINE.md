# Advertising campaign doctrine

This doctrine owns campaign-specific business and evidence rules. It is subordinate to `AGENTS.md`; the executable packet contract is `campaign.v1.json`. Installing this domain does not authorize advertising activity.

1. Start from the business objective, customer need, offer, and available evidence. Recover known context once; ask for material missing decisions only.
2. Keep supported facts, audience hypotheses, creative ideas, and measured results distinguishable. Claims require support and retained qualifications. Never invent testimonials, scarcity, offers, research, or outcomes.
3. Preserve brand voice while testing meaningfully different concepts. Keep the ad's promise, offer terms, CTA, and destination consistent.
4. Bind every working artifact to a campaign, version, owner, source, and dependency. Reuse the existing workspace. A document workflow is valid; no repository or paid tool is required.
5. Plan within a stated currency, budget ceiling, period, and channel constraints. Unknown budget means provisional planning, never implied permission to spend.
6. Keep production readiness, review, authorization, observed launch, and measured performance separate. Existing explicit permission remains valid for its unchanged scope. A material change reopens affected checks.
7. Verify current channel requirements from primary sources when needed. Record unresolved format, rights, platform, or jurisdiction questions at their actual stage; do not manufacture universal requirements or claim specialist approval.
8. Predefine the objective, measurement source, comparison, window, and decision rule. Show counts alongside rates, handle missing/zero denominators honestly, and distinguish attributed outcomes from causal lift or profit.
9. Improve through bounded, evidence-bearing iterations. Preserve prior results and successful assets. No data yields a collection or instrumentation task, not invented optimization.
10. Deliver the user-facing output cleanly. Keep source mechanics and internal working notes separate from consumer-facing ad copy. Produce actual artifacts where tools allow; label briefs, drafts, and unavailable runtime proof accurately.

Primary references checked on 2026-09-09: [Google Ads misrepresentation policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6020955?hl=en-GB) informs the supported-claims boundary; [Google Ads experiment monitoring](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6318747?hl=en) illustrates why performance conclusions need experiment evidence. These are references, not assumed platform choices for this user. Selected channels require current checks when a real campaign is specified.
